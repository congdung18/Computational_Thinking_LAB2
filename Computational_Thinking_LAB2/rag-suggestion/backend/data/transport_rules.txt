# Weather vs Transportation Rules

## Sunny Weather
- If the weather is sunny and the distance is short (less than 2 km), Walking is highly recommended for health and enjoyment.
- If the weather is sunny and the distance is moderate (2 km to 10 km), Biking is the best option for speed and fresh air.
- Users who prefer scenic routes should walk or bike when it's sunny.
- If the distance is long (greater than 10 km) and it's sunny, taking a Car or Motorbike is recommended to save time. Public transportation is also a viable option.

## Rainy Weather
- If it is raining heavily, Walking and Biking are completely prohibited. Cars or Public Transportation are the safest and most comfortable options.
- If there is light rain and the user has an umbrella or raincoat (user preference: prepared), Walking for short distances is acceptable, but Biking should be avoided due to slippery roads. Motorbikes are okay but less comfortable.
- When raining, Public Transportation might take longer due to traffic, but it keeps the user dry.

## Snowy/Icy Weather
- In snowy conditions, Biking and Motorbikes are extremely dangerous and should never be recommended.
- Walking is okay for very short distances if the user wears winter boots.
- Public transportation is the safest option as city vehicles are usually equipped for snow.
- Cars are acceptable only if they have winter tires and the user is comfortable driving in snow.

## Windy/Stormy Weather
- During high winds, Biking is very difficult and not recommended.
- Motorbikes are also dangerous in stormy/windy conditions.
- Cars or Public Transportation are the best choices.

## User Preferences Integration
- If a user specifies a preference for "exercise" or "health", prioritize Walking or Biking unless weather strictly forbids it (e.g., heavy rain, snow, storm).
- If a user prefers "speed" or "comfort", prioritize Car or Motorbike.
- If a user wants "cheap" or "budget-friendly", prioritize Walking, Biking, or Public Transportation.
- If a user prefers "eco-friendly" options, suggest Walking, Biking, or Public Transportation over Cars and Motorbikes.
